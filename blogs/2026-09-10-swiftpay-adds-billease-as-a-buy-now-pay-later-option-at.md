Swiftpay today announced that Billease is now available as a buy now, pay later (BNPL) payment method in its online checkout. Merchants already accepting payments through Swiftpay can enable Billease from their existing setup, and it appears as a payment option to shoppers at checkout. The partnership works in both directions.
